## About

gitget is a small team from Bosnia and Herzegovina that designs and builds web and mobile applications with AI at their core. We take ideas from the first sketch to a product that is live, fast and easy to use.

## What we do

- **Web apps:** fast, responsive products, from landing pages to full platforms
- **Mobile apps:** cross-platform apps for Android and iOS
- **AI features:** assistants, smart search, recommendations and automation built into the product
- **Design to launch:** UX, interface design, development, deployment and care after launch

## How we work

1. **Idea:** we listen and define what the product needs to do
2. **First version:** a working prototype early, so decisions are made on something real
3. **Polish:** we refine the details together until it feels right
4. **Launch and care:** the product goes live and keeps improving
